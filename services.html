Common Questions

Questions About Working With CliqLabs

Which package should I start with?
Start with a Project Sprint if you have one specific problem to solve. Choose Dedicated Expert if you need consistent monthly capacity. Choose Full Backend Team if you want CliqLabs running setup, automation, support and branding together under your name.
Can I move between packages later?
Yes. Many agencies start with a Project Sprint, move to Dedicated Expert once the workload becomes ongoing, and upgrade to Full Backend Team as client count grows.
Do the packages include support for our clients, or just our own account?
Project Sprint and Dedicated Expert focus on your own account and build work. Full Backend Team can include white-label support for your clients under your agency's brand.
Is there a contract?
No long-term contract is required on any package. Scope and hours are agreed before work starts, and you can adjust or pause between billing periods.
What if we need something that doesn't fit neatly into one package?
Book a call and describe the bottleneck. We will recommend the closest package and adjust scope where it genuinely doesn't fit.
